01 / CONTEXT & SETTING

Bringing applied AI to commercial lending and credit risk.

My current role at Infosys focuses on risk-modelling and credit-risk analysis solutions within commercial financial services. The work includes next-generation commercial lending initiatives that connect predictive statistical models and generative AI across the complete delivery lifecycle—from initial solution design to production deployment.

Commercial lending operations deal with high-value underwriting decisions where transparency, repeatability, and regulatory auditability are non-negotiable. Modernizing these systems requires bridging quantitative credit scores with unstructured borrower evidence while guaranteeing explainability for credit underwriters.

FRAGMENTED UNDERWRITING

Siloed scores & manual document lookup

Numerical risk scores and qualitative borrower disclosures lived in separate silos, requiring manual cross-referencing across filings, covenants, and footnotes.

CONVERGED DECISION FLOW

Deterministic risk baseline + grounded GenAI

Connects statistical risk models with citation-backed generative synthesis so underwriters inspect quantitative ratios alongside linked filing evidence.

03 / ARCHITECTURE & SIGNALS

Balancing statistical rigor with explainable reasoning.

Traditional credit scoring relies on rigid numerical models (such as logistic regressions or gradient-boosted decision trees) that struggle with qualitative disclosures, audited financial footnotes, or macroeconomic commentary. Conversely, generative models produce expressive narrative explanations but can hallucinate numbers if not grounded by strict constraints.

The system architecture addresses this by decoupling quantitative risk & Economic Capital calculation from citation-grounded GenAI evidence synthesis:
