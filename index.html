02 / HOW I WORK

From fuzzy problem
to useful product.

Good engineering starts before the first line of code — and keeps going after the first deployment.

  1. 01

    Understand

    Get close to the user, workflow, and constraints.

  2. 02

    Design

    Choose a small, testable path through the problem.

  3. 03

    Build

    Connect data, models, services, and experience.

  4. 04

    Evaluate

    Measure quality, inspect failures, and iterate.

  5. 05

    Deliver

    Make it deployable, observable, and usable.

↳

Especially for AI: a convincing demo is a start, not the finish line. Grounding, evaluation, failure handling, and the workflow around the model matter just as much.
