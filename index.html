ANZ Hackfest 2024 · Top 20 Finalist

Digital Trust with AI

A synthetic-identity verification concept combining Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) and multimodal signal analysis to strengthen digital onboarding trust.

GANsMultimodal SignalsDeep LearningFraud Triage

The problem

Financial institutions face increasingly sophisticated synthetic identities that blend real and fabricated attributes across documents, biometrics, and behavioral signals.

Technical approach

Designed a multi-signal verification pipeline exploring adversarial pattern detection (GANs) alongside cross-modal consistency checks to surface high-risk anomalies for human review.

Outcome

Selected as a Top 20 Finalist at ANZ Hackfest 2024 for connecting deep-learning fraud signals to an explainable verification workflow.

Legacy Code Modernization

A GenAI-assisted engineering workflow designed to interpret, document, and modernize legacy Assembly codebases with human-in-the-loop verification.

GenAI Code TranslationAssembly AnalysisHuman-in-the-Loop1st Place Winner

The challenge

Core enterprise logic trapped in low-level Assembly routines is difficult to maintain or migrate because register-level control flow obscures business intent.

System workflow

Combined structured prompting and code-comprehension stages to extract control-flow intent first, then generate readable modern target code alongside side-by-side review notes.

Recognition

Won 1st place in an organization-wide GenAI modernization hackathon for practical developer ergonomics and inspectable output quality.
